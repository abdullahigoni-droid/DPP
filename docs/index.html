Installation Guidance

  • Fill centre housing with clean engine oil before installation and crank without starting to prime oil feed.
  • Replace or verify the oil feed pipe and banjo bolt before fitment. Do not install onto a contaminated oil system.
  • Idle for 3 minutes on first start. Avoid high boost and engine speeds above 2,500 RPM for the first 50 km.

Torque Specifications

Oil feed banjo bolt 22 Nm Use new copper washers
Turbine housing V-clamp 10 Nm Tighten evenly
Turbo mounting studs (M8) 28 Nm Apply anti-seize compound

Handling Warnings

  • Do not fit before resolving oil starvation or boost-control faults
  • Do not reuse contaminated oil feed lines
  • Do not run the engine immediately after fitment without oil priming

Warranty

Commercial warranty subject to The Turbo Guy sale, returns, and installation requirements.

Warranty support requires correct installation, oil feed inspection/replacement where required, pre-lubrication, and an installation record. Oil starvation, contamination, and foreign object damage are excluded.
